Chinese Flash

Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 6, 2026

1. Overview

Chinese Flash ("we", "our", or "the app") is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we handle information in connection with your use of the Chinese Flash iOS application.

Chinese Flash does not collect, store, transmit, or share any personal data. The app operates entirely offline on your device.

2. Information Collection

We do not collect any information from you. The app does not gather, record, or transmit any data of any kind, including but not limited to:

  • Personal identification information (name, email, phone number, etc.)
  • Location data
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or crash reports
  • Contact lists, photos, or media files
  • Financial or payment information
  • Search history or browsing activity
  • Health or fitness data

The app does not require you to create an account, register, or provide any personal information to use any feature.

3. Data Storage

All data generated by your use of the app -- including your study progress, flashcard ratings, bookmarked words, custom words, and achievement records -- is stored exclusively on your device using local storage mechanisms (iOS filesystem / UserDefaults).

This data:

  • Never leaves your device
  • Is not uploaded to any server or cloud service
  • Is not accessible to us or any third party
  • Is automatically deleted when you delete the app from your device

4. Network Access

The app does not require network access and does not make any network requests. All features of the app function completely offline. The app does not contain any code for making HTTP requests, API calls, or any form of network communication.

5. Third-Party Services

The app does not integrate with any third-party SDKs, services, or frameworks that collect data. Specifically, the app does not include:

  • Analytics services (e.g., Google Analytics, Firebase Analytics)
  • Crash reporting services (e.g., Crashlytics, Bugly)
  • Advertising networks (e.g., AdMob, Facebook Audience Network)
  • Social media SDKs (e.g., Facebook, WeChat, Twitter)
  • Push notification services
  • Payment processing SDKs
  • Any other third-party tracking or data collection tools

The only system framework used for speech synthesis is Apple's built-in AVSpeechSynthesizer, which operates on-device and does not transmit data to external servers.

6. Device Permissions

The app does not request any device permissions. It does not access your camera, microphone, photo library, contacts, location, or any other sensitive system resource.

7. Children's Privacy

Chinese Flash is rated 4+ on the App Store and is suitable for users of all ages, including children.

Consistent with our overall privacy practices, the app does not collect any personal information from any users, including children under the age of 13. We comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA) by not collecting, storing, or sharing any data from children.

Parents and guardians can feel confident that their children's privacy is fully protected when using this app, as no data of any kind is collected or transmitted.

8. Data Security

Because all data is stored locally on your device and never transmitted over any network, the security of your data is tied to the security of your device itself. We recommend keeping your device protected with a passcode or biometric authentication.

If you delete the app from your device, all app data stored locally will be permanently removed by the operating system.

9. Your Rights

Since we do not collect any personal data, there is no personal data to access, modify, or delete from our side. However, you have full control over all data stored locally within the app:

  • You can view and modify all your study data within the app at any time
  • You can delete custom words and bookmarks from within the app
  • You can delete all app data by removing the app from your device

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make any material changes, we will update the "Last Updated" date at the top of this page. We encourage you to review this policy periodically.

As of this writing, the app collects no data, and any future changes to this policy would only occur if the app's data practices change. Any such change would be communicated through an app update.
