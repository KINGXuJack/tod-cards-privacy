Need help with Draw & Guess? Find answers to common questions below.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I play?

One person takes the phone, taps a card to reveal the secret word, then draws it on paper or a whiteboard while others guess. Tap "Guessed" when the team gets it right, or "Skip" to move on.

Does the app require internet?

No! Draw & Guess works 100% offline. All 500+ words are built into the app. No Wi-Fi or cellular connection needed.

Can I create my own word categories?

Yes! Go to the Library tab, tap "Create Custom Category," give it a name, and add your own words. Great for themed parties, inside jokes, or classroom use.

How do I change the number of rounds?

Go to Settings > Rounds Per Game, then select 10, 15, 20, 25, 30, or 50 rounds.

What do the difficulty levels mean?

Easy uses simpler, everyday words. Hard includes more abstract concepts and challenging vocabulary. Mixed combines both for a balanced experience.

Is the app available in Chinese?

Yes! Draw & Guess supports both English and Simplified Chinese. The language follows your device settings.

How do I clear my game history?

Go to Settings > Content Management > Clear Game Records. Note: this action cannot be undone.
