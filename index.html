Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 29, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how TOD Cards ("the App") handles your information. We take your privacy seriously and are committed to protecting your personal data.

1. Information We Do NOT Collect

We want to be clear: TOD Cards does NOT collect, store, or transmit any of the following:

  • Personal information (name, email, phone number, address)
  • Location data or GPS information
  • Device identifiers or advertising identifiers
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Crash reports or diagnostic data sent to external servers
  • Financial information or payment data
  • Photos, contacts, or files from your device

2. Information Stored Locally

The App stores the following data exclusively on your device using iOS UserDefaults:

  • Favorite Questions: Questions you have saved as favorites during gameplay
  • Game Records: History of questions drawn during each game session
  • Scene Configuration: Your selected party scenes and intensity level preferences

None of this data is ever transmitted off your device. If you delete the App, all locally stored data is permanently removed.

3. Device Permissions

TOD Cards does NOT request any device permissions. The app does not need access to your camera, photo library, contacts, location, microphone, or any other sensitive permissions. All game content is built into the app.

4. Third-Party Services

TOD Cards does NOT use any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or crash reporting tools. The App is entirely self-contained and does not communicate with any external servers.

5. Data Security

All data is stored locally on your device using Apple's built-in storage mechanisms (UserDefaults). This data benefits from iOS's built-in security protections, including device encryption and app sandboxing.

6. Children's Privacy

TOD Cards is rated 17+ on the App Store due to the "Spicy" intensity level which may contain mature themes. We recommend parental guidance for users under 17. The App does not knowingly collect any personal information from children.

7. Content Standards

TOD Cards is designed for entertainment purposes only. The questions and challenges are intended for consensual party gameplay among adults. Users are encouraged to:

  • Respect other players' boundaries and comfort levels
  • Skip any question or challenge they are uncomfortable with
  • Use the "Mild" intensity level for family-friendly gameplay
  • Stop playing at any time if the game becomes uncomfortable
The developers of TOD Cards are not responsible for how users choose to interpret or act on the content in the app.

8. Your Rights

Since all data is stored locally on your device:

  • Access: You can view all your data within the App (Favorites, Game Records, Settings)
  • Delete: You can clear your favorites and game records from the Settings page within the App
  • Control: You can delete the App at any time, which permanently removes all stored data

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last updated" date.
