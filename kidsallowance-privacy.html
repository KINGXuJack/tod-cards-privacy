Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 7, 2026

Summary: KidsAllowance does not collect any personal data. All your family's allowance and chore records are stored locally on your device only.

1. Data Collection

KidsAllowance does not collect any of the following:

  • Personal identification information
  • Children's personal information
  • Location data
  • Usage analytics or tracking data
  • Device identifiers
  • Any other personal information

2. Local Data Storage

All data generated by KidsAllowance, including:

  • Children's profiles (names, avatars, birthdays)
  • Allowance settings and balances
  • Chore assignments and completion records
  • Savings goals and progress
  • Transaction history

...is stored exclusively on your device using SwiftData local storage. This data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to export it.

3. No Third-Party Services

KidsAllowance does not integrate any third-party SDKs, analytics services, advertising networks, or cloud services. The app operates completely offline after installation.

4. Children's Privacy

KidsAllowance is designed to help parents manage their children's allowances. Since all data is stored locally and no personal information is collected or transmitted, children's privacy is fully protected.

5. Data Export and Import

You can export transaction history as CSV through the app's Ledger feature. This exported file can be shared or saved externally. You have full control over this data.

6. Data Deletion

You can delete all data at any time through Settings. Additionally, deleting the app from your device will remove all associated data.

7. Changes to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. Any changes will be reflected in the "Last Updated" date above.

8. Contact Us

If you have any questions about this privacy policy, please contact us through the app's Settings > Feedback section.
