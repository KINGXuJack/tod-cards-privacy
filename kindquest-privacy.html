Data Storage

All data generated by KindQuest is stored exclusively on your device using Apple's SwiftData framework. This means:

  • ✅ Your data is stored locally in a SwiftData persistent store on your iPhone or iPad.
  • ✅ No cloud backup or synchronization is performed by KindQuest.
  • ✅ Data is protected by iOS's built-in app sandbox and file system encryption.
  • ✅ If you delete the app, all KindQuest data is permanently removed from your device.
  • ✅ No data is accessible to other apps on your device due to iOS sandboxing.

What is SwiftData? SwiftData is Apple's native data persistence framework for iOS. It stores data locally within the app's sandboxed container on your device. The data never leaves your device unless you explicitly choose to back up your device through iCloud or iTunes -- and even then, the data remains encrypted and private.

Your Rights

Because KindQuest does not collect any personal data, there is no data for you to access, correct, delete, or port. However, you maintain full control over the data stored locally on your device:

  • 📱 Delete Your Data: Simply delete the KindQuest app from your device. All data associated with the app will be permanently and irreversibly removed.
