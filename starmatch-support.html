Frequently Asked Questions

What is StarMatch?

StarMatch is an iOS app that lets you explore zodiac compatibility. Enter two signs — or use a birthday to auto-detect a sign — and instantly see a compatibility score across Love, Friendship, and Work dimensions. It also delivers daily tips tailored to your sign and lets you share results as beautiful cards.

What are the 12 zodiac signs?

The twelve signs of the Western (tropical) zodiac are:

  • Aries — March 21 – April 19
  • Taurus — April 20 – May 20
  • Gemini — May 21 – June 20
  • Cancer — June 21 – July 22
  • Leo — July 23 – August 22
  • Virgo — August 23 – September 22
  • Libra — September 23 – October 22
  • Scorpio — October 23 – November 21
  • Sagittarius — November 22 – December 21
  • Capricorn — December 22 – January 19
  • Aquarius — January 20 – February 18
  • Pisces — February 19 – March 20
How does compatibility scoring work?

Each pair of signs is evaluated across three dimensions — Love, Friendship, and Work — and assigned a score. The scores are based on traditional astrological principles, including element compatibility (fire, earth, air, water), modality (cardinal, fixed, mutable), and well-established sign relationships. A higher score indicates stronger natural harmony between two signs in that particular area.

What do the three score dimensions mean?
  • Love — Measures romantic and emotional chemistry between two signs. High scores suggest natural attraction, mutual understanding, and long-term romantic potential.
  • Friendship — Rates how well two signs get along as friends. High scores indicate easy conversation, shared interests, and reliable companionship.
  • Work — Evaluates professional compatibility. High scores mean the two signs complement each other in teamwork, communication style, and problem-solving approaches.

Each dimension is scored independently, so a pair might have a high Friendship score but a lower Love score — and that is completely normal.

How do I find my zodiac sign?

The simplest way is to enter your birth date in StarMatch. The app automatically determines your zodiac sign based on the standard Western zodiac date ranges. You can also look up the date ranges manually (see the question above) and select your sign directly.

Can I check compatibility between any two signs?

Yes. You can freely select any two zodiac signs — including the same sign compared with itself — and view the full compatibility breakdown across all three dimensions. There are no restrictions or locked pairings.

How many unique sign pairings are included?

StarMatch includes all 78 unique pairings of the 12 zodiac signs. This covers every possible combination (including each sign paired with itself), calculated using the formula n(n+1)/2 = 12 × 13 / 2 = 78.

What are daily tips?

Every day, StarMatch provides a personalized tip tailored to your zodiac sign. These tips cover topics such as relationships, self-reflection, communication, and personal growth. New tips are available daily, and your history of past tips is saved so you can revisit them at any time.

Do I need an account or login?

No. StarMatch does not require any account, registration, or login. You can start using the app immediately after installing it. There are no usernames, passwords, or email addresses involved.

Does the app require internet?

No. StarMatch is fully functional offline. All compatibility data, daily tips, and features are built into the app and stored locally on your device. You do not need an internet connection to use any part of the app.

Is my data private?

Absolutely. StarMatch stores all data — including your saved sign, compatibility check history, and tip history — locally on your device. Nothing is transmitted to any server. The app does not collect, track, or share any personal information. Your data never leaves your phone.

How do I share my compatibility results?

After viewing a compatibility result, tap the Share button. StarMatch generates a beautifully designed result card that you can send via Messages, email, social media, or any other sharing option available on your device.

Can I revisit previous compatibility checks?

Yes. StarMatch keeps a local history of all the compatibility checks you have performed. You can browse this history at any time to review past results without needing to re-select the signs.
