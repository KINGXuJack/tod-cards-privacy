Frequently Asked Questions

Q: Does TOD Cards require an internet connection?

No. All questions and game content are built into the app. TOD Cards works completely offline — perfect for parties anywhere, even without Wi-Fi.

Q: How many questions are in the app?

TOD Cards includes hundreds of Truth and Dare questions across 5 party scenes (Friends, Couples, Family, Team Building, Strangers) and 2 intensity levels (Mild and Spicy), in both English and Chinese.

Q: What's the difference between Mild and Spicy?

Mild questions are light-hearted and family-friendly — great for mixed groups or when you want to keep things fun and safe. Spicy questions go deeper and are better suited for close friends or adults who are comfortable with more personal topics.

Q: Can I save questions I like?

Yes! Swipe right on any card or tap the heart icon to save it to your Favorites. You can browse all saved questions in the Favorites tab anytime.

Q: How do I change the language?

TOD Cards automatically detects your device's language. If your device is set to English, the app displays in English. If set to Chinese, it displays in Chinese. Change your device language in Settings → General → Language & Region.

Q: Is my data private?

Absolutely. All favorites, game records, and settings are stored only on your device. No data is ever sent to any server. We do not collect any personal information, use analytics, or show ads.

Q: How do I clear my game history?

Go to Settings → Clear Data. You can clear favorites, game records, or reset all settings individually.

Q: Can I use TOD Cards for team building at work?

Yes! The "Team Building" scene is designed for ice-breaking and group bonding. We recommend using the "Mild" intensity level for professional settings.

Q: I found a question inappropriate. What should I do?

Simply swipe left to skip any question you're uncomfortable with. You can also contact us with feedback, and we'll review it for future updates.
